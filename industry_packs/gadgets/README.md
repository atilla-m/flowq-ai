# Gadget pack data

The pack contains 80 catalog SKUs: 44 phones, 16 laptops, 8 tablets, 4 watches, 5 headphones and 3 consoles. There are 40 exact-model accessories, 20 synthetic customers, 10 seeded order records, and three synthetic Baku branches. All customer-facing text is English; district names and AZN remain unchanged.

Each SKU represents one storage/RAM/color configuration. `colors` lists the color of that specific SKU; it does not imply that other colors are in stock. Top-level `storage` and `color` preserve existing clients. `specs` includes storage in GB, RAM in GB, CPU and screen. Null means not applicable or not confirmed from the referenced public specifications; the agent must not infer a value. For devices without usable storage the legacy top-level value is 0. Regional CPUs/configurations vary: this pack deliberately identifies its chosen SKU variant, including Exynos Galaxy S24 and 4G Redmi/A-series variants.

`price_azn` values are static, rounded estimates of Baku shop retail pricing, not live retailer offers or scraped availability. Existing demo SKUs used by tests/evals keep their prices; newer SKU ranges were checked against Baku retailers on 2026-10-09. Stock and warranty are FlowQ demo settings. The branches, 14-day return terms and 0% 3/6/12-month installments are fictional FlowQ store policies, not claims about retailer policies or Azerbaijani law.

Price references include [iSpace's iPhone 17 listing](https://ispace.az/ru/product/iphone-17-256-gb-black-mg6j4af-a), [İrşad's iPhone 17 listing](https://irshad.az/az/mehsullar/iphone-17-256-gb-mist-blue), [İrşad's MacBook Air offer](https://irshad.az/az/mehsul-icmali/daha-agilli-daha-sueretli-macbook-air), [Laptopiya's TUF laptop listing](https://laptopiya.az/products/asus-tuf-gaming-f16-fx607vj-rl006-90nr0mz6-m00060) and [İrşad's console catalog](https://irshad.az/neqliyyat-ve-eylence/oyun-konsollari/sony). These establish representative local price ranges rather than a verified quotation for every SKU.

Trade-in base values cover every phone, tablet and laptop in the catalog, plus legacy iPhone 11/12 and Galaxy S21/S22. They are deterministic demo buying offers; the rules apply model+storage and observed condition. RAM does not alter the demo valuation. A Plus model remains distinct from the base model. For devices without Face ID, the checklist records `face_id_working=true` as not applicable. For non-Apple devices, `icloud_signed_out=true` means activation/account locks have been removed. Images must establish battery health, not charge level.

Customer `orders` are seed records imported into SQLite once. Open processing/unpaid orders reserve stock once; delivered/returned history does not consume current stock. Seeded earlier trade-ins are explicitly historical and cannot fund a new order. Seeds never create a paid status: unpaid orders have a pending ledger, and other historical orders report no recorded payment evidence. Keep IDs stable to preserve idempotent startup and any subsequent payment of the unpaid demo order.

Accessory images are local SVG placeholders labeled with the exact compatible model. `image_url` uses the development base URL in the JSON; the backend rewrites it with `BACKEND_PUBLIC_URL` when returning tool results. Branch selection is a configured service-district assignment, not a geographic distance or current-open calculation.
